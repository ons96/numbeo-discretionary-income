How this is calculated

Every number comes from Numbeo, fetched in USD and converted to the currency you pick.

  • Net salary — Numbeo's “Average Monthly Net Salary (After Tax)” × 12.
  • Living costs — Numbeo's “estimated monthly costs for a single person, excluding rent” × 12. Where that figure hasn't been collected for a city yet, it is marked ≈ and estimated from Numbeo's cost-of-living index (the same basket, scaled from New York).
  • Rent — either a whole 1-bedroom apartment, or a room in a 3-bedroom flat (a third of the 3-bedroom rent), in the city centre or outside it.
  • Left over — salary − living costs − rent, per year.

Numbeo figures are crowd-sourced averages, skew towards people who fill in such sites, and can lag reality. A city with a handful of contributors is noise, not truth — treat the tail of the list as a starting point, not a verdict.
